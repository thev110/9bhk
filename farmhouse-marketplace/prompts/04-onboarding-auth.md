## UX
- Keep onboarding skippable.
- Login should be visually light and photography-led.
- Never block public discovery just because the user is unauthenticated.
- Require authentication at the appropriate private action boundary (save/book/list).

Acceptance: flows work on mobile and desktop, focus states are accessible, loading/error states exist.
