# Prompt 11 — Host Listing Wizard

Build a multi-step mobile-first listing wizard.

## Steps
1. Property basics
2. Location
3. Capacity
4. Amenities
5. Photos
6. Pricing
7. Availability
8. Preview
9. Publish

## Step 1 copy
`Tell us about your farmhouse`
Fields: name, short description, property type, maximum guests.

## Step 2
`Where is your farmhouse?`
Location picker + address details.

## Step 3
Guests, bedrooms, beds, bathrooms.

## Step 4
Pool, BBQ, bonfire, Wi-Fi, AC, parking, kitchen, pet friendly, indoor games, outdoor games, projector/music, caretaker, power backup.

## Requirements
- autosave draft
- clear step progress
- validation per step
- back/next navigation
- resume incomplete draft
- no data loss on accidental navigation

Stop after this task.
