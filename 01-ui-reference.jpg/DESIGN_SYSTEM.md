# Design System

## Colors
- Forest: `#18352B`
- Deep Forest: `#10251E`
- Sage: `#7C927E`
- Moss: `#556B5D`
- Cream: `#F7F3EA`
- Warm White: `#FCFBF8`
- Sand: `#E9DFCC`
- Terracotta: `#C86B45`
- Ink: `#181A18`
- Muted: `#70756F`

## Typography
- UI: Manrope
- Editorial display accent: Instrument Serif, sparingly

## Radius
- 10 / 16 / 24 / 32px
- pill: 999px

## Principles
1. Mobile first at 390x844.
2. Photography is the hero.
3. One primary action per screen.
4. Progressive disclosure for filters/forms.
5. Minimum touch target: 44px.
6. Soft borders, restrained shadows.
7. Do not fill every surface with cards.
8. Motion communicates state; it does not decorate everything.
9. Do not use green on every icon; preserve green for actions/status/brand emphasis.
10. Desktop is an enhancement of the mobile composition, not a different product.
